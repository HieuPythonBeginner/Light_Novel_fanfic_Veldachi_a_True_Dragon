## Appearance (Full Description – Final Design)

Velchthonic appears as an extremely tall, powerfully built bipedal dragon who stands significantly taller than Veldachi and clearly surpasses even Veldora in height and sheer presence. His overall silhouette is imposing, elongated, and heavily muscled.

The majority of his body is covered in smooth, deep jet-black scales. Over these scales grows thick, segmented golden natural plating that covers the chest, abdomen, shoulders, outer arms, outer thighs, shins, and the entire length of his long, heavy tail. The golden plating has a sharp, refined, almost metallic appearance with pointed ridges and spikes, especially at the shoulders, elbows, knees, and along the tail. It is not worn armor. It is natural biological plating grown directly from his body.

His head is covered by an elaborate golden bone-carapace fused seamlessly with his skull. Multiple long, sharp horns curve upward and backward in an asymmetric fan, framing his head like a crown, with jagged serrated ridges running between them. Two blade-like golden fin-plates flare outward from the sides of his head. There is absolutely no hair anywhere on his head or body.

His facial features are sharp and feral. The lower part of the face is covered by a pale, almost bone-white muzzle and jaw, revealing rows of sharp teeth even when the mouth is closed. His eyes glow a vivid, piercing red with a cold, sadistic intensity. A single large purple gem is embedded at the center of his chest, and another smaller purple gem sits higher on the collar area where the cape is fastened.

Velchthonic wears a long, tattered black cape with a deep crimson-red inner lining. The cape is heavily damaged, with large ragged tears and bloodstains, hanging loosely over his shoulders and flowing down his back. The fabric appears scorched and battle-worn. The cape physically responds to movement and residual force, trailing behind him with heavy, deliberate weight.

His entire body is covered in fresh and dried blood splatters, streaks, and dripping marks, as if he has just finished a massacre. The blood coats the golden plating, the black scales, and the cape indiscriminately.

**Critical detail – Thighs and lower body:**  
The golden plating on his outer thighs is broken and damaged in several places. On the left thigh in particular, the plating is cracked open, revealing exposed white bone and deep puncture wounds. Blood continues to seep from these injuries, running down the leg. The wounds appear intentional or the result of prolonged self-inflicted or battle damage that he does not bother to heal fully, treating them as a permanent part of his appearance.

His hands end in long, sharp golden claws. His feet are digitigrade with matching golden claws. A long, thick, black-and-gold scaled tail extends behind him, moving with heavy, deliberate weight.

The overall posture is upright, dominant, and slightly arrogant. He radiates an aura of absolute menace and casual cruelty.

## Past

Velchthonic’s history began long before the existence of Veldachi.

Unlike Veldachi, who was born directly within Veldanava’s creation and gradually grew into a young True Dragon surrounded by family, Velchthonic had already existed for an incomprehensibly long period of time before Veldachi ever appeared.

At some point during that ancient existence, Velchthonic became fascinated by the existence of Veldachi and the strange happiness surrounding him. Veldachi had family. He had siblings. He had a place within Veldanava’s creation. He was loved, protected, teased, and remembered.

Velchthonic wanted to experience something similar.

So, despite being fundamentally different from Veldachi, Velchthonic deliberately created a new existence modeled after the same general origin and nature that had produced Veldachi. He attempted to create a version of himself that could exist within the same kind of world, hoping that by becoming something similar to Veldachi, he could finally experience the same sense of belonging.

It did not work.

The new existence was treated differently from Veldachi from the very beginning.

The people around him were distant.

The ancient beings who should have recognized him as something important remained indifferent.

Those who surrounded Veldachi with affection did not give Velchthonic the same warmth. His attempts to approach others were met with silence, hesitation, discomfort, or simple disregard.

He continued trying anyway.

He imitated the behaviors he saw in Veldachi. He attempted to form relationships. He tried to understand happiness, friendship, family, and affection. He waited for someone to notice that he was also there.

No one came.

As the ages passed, the loneliness became heavier.

Velchthonic slowly stopped trying to imitate Veldachi.

He stopped asking to be accepted.

He stopped believing that someone would eventually understand him.

What began as loneliness gradually became isolation. Isolation became resentment. Resentment became emptiness.

Eventually, something inside Velchthonic broke.

He no longer wanted the world to acknowledge him.

He wanted the world to disappear.

The first act of destruction was small.

The second was deliberate.

The third became irreversible.

Velchthonic began destroying the realities surrounding him one after another. Worlds, dimensions, civilizations, timelines, concepts, and entire layers of existence were gradually consumed by his growing destructive power.

The beings who had once ignored him finally noticed him.

But by then, it was too late.

Their fear was the first genuine emotion they had ever shown toward him.

Velchthonic did not find satisfaction in it.

He found confirmation.

They had been capable of noticing him all along.

They simply needed a reason to fear him.

The destruction continued.

Entire realities collapsed into The Endless. Timelines were severed. Dimensions were erased. Memories of civilizations disappeared alongside the civilizations themselves. Even the structures that defined existence began to collapse under Velchthonic’s power.

Eventually, the catastrophe reached a point where the destruction could no longer be contained within individual worlds.

Reality itself began to die.

Velchthonic watched it happen in silence.

There was no celebration.

No rage.

No triumphant declaration.

Only an ancient, exhausted existence standing alone at the end of everything he had once wanted to belong to.

The destruction of the surrounding reality ultimately became the foundation of The Endless as it exists today.

Velchthonic remained.

Everything else was gone.

That was the moment he stopped searching for a place within reality.

He had become something that existed outside the need for one.
